\section{Selected Applied Projects}
\project{Financial Risk Meter for China}{Systemic risk / network analytics}
{Designed a China-focused systemic-risk indicator using tail-event dependence and quantile-Lasso methods to identify risk transmission among major financial institutions and macro-financial drivers.}

\project{FRM for Crypto Markets}{Digital assets / market risk}
{Extended network-risk measurement to cryptocurrency markets, linking Financial Risk Meter signals with pricing-kernel volatility and forward-looking market risk.}

\project{Carbon Risk Dynamic Indicator (CARDI)}{Climate finance / ESG analytics}
{Built a Quantile-LASSO network indicator to measure carbon-risk transmission, volatility-demand shifts and low-carbon premia across financial markets.}

\project{Ethereum Development Milestones}{Digital assets / event analytics}
{Evaluated market responses to Ethereum protocol upgrades, including return, volatility and spillover effects around major development milestones.}

\project{Bond-Market Default Risk and Resource Allocation}{Credit risk / causal inference}
{Studied how rigid payment expectations, implicit guarantees and default-risk networks affect bond-market pricing efficiency and financial-resource allocation.}
